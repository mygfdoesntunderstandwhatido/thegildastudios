ABOUT · WHY THE GILDA STUDIOS

It started with a tapa in Seville.

One day we were in Seville, enjoying tapas and listening to live music, talking about what was next and how to grow the business. Then the waiter dropped off our first tapa: the gilda.

01 · THE GILDA

A gilda is a Spanish tapa: a skewer and three simple ingredients. A green olive, a salt-cured Cantabrian anchovy and a pickled guindilla pepper.

One skewer was holding three completely different ingredients, and together they made one balanced tapa. That was the studio we wanted to build.
