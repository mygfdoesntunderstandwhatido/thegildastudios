# Accessibility (#29)

The site targets WCAG 2.2 AA. This page records how that is checked, the
contrast of every colour pair in use, the keyboard run-through and the screen
reader notes for each interactive component.

## How it is checked

`npm run test:a11y` runs `tests/a11y/a11y.spec.ts` against the production
build, and CI runs it on every PR after the performance checks. It fails the
build when any of these breaks:

| Check                                                                                                         | Pages and widths                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA, plus best practice) reports no violations of any impact                  | All 10 public pages, at 390 and 1280      |
| Exactly one `h1`, one `main#main` and a footer                                                                | All 10 public pages, at 390 and 1280      |
| **Pepper is never text on Cream, Sand, Paper or Pepper pale** (the brand rule, checked on rendered colours)   | All 10 public pages, at 390 and 1280      |
| Every tab stop draws a focus ring at least 2px wide with 3:1 contrast against the ground it sits on           | All 10 public pages, at 390 and 1280      |
| Text keeps AA contrast while each link, button and choice is hovered                                          | Home, Services, Projects, Contact at 1280 |
| No horizontal scrolling at 320px (reflow, and 400% zoom of a 1280 window)                                     | All 10 public pages                       |
| No transitions or animations under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`                                           | All 10 public pages                       |
| Keyboard paths: hero mark, branch strip, rotator, disclosures, pillars, filters, mobile menu, form, skip link | The page each feature lives on            |

The pepper check was proven by setting the shared label colour to pepper: the
test failed on every page with a light-ground label, then passed again once
the colour was restored.

Lighthouse CI now requires an Accessibility score of 100 on the six audited
pages (it was 95). The latest run scores 100 on all of them; see
[the performance record](performance.md).

The ten public pages are `/`, `/about/`, `/services/`, `/projects/`, the three
case studies, `/contact/`, `/contact/thanks/` and the 404 page.

## What changed in this pass

- **Olive text on Sand.** Brand olive `#646E2C` is 4.27:1 on Sand and on
  Pepper pale: under AA for the 10–12px mono labels. Olive _text_ on those
  grounds now uses `--olive-text-on-sand` (`#5E6729`, the same hue 6% darker,
  4.71:1). Components set text with `var(--olive-text)`, which is brand olive
  everywhere except inside Sand and Pepper pale bands, the Sand header and
  menu, and the Services jump cards. Fills, rules, dots and buttons keep the
  brand olive. This lifted Lighthouse Accessibility on home, About and
  Services from 95–96 to 100, and let the case-study "next" sector label go
  back to the design's olive.
- **Hover states.** The design fades hovered elements to 86% (75% for links on
  dark), but the brand readme says transparency is never for text, and the
  fade took text under AA (Cream on an olive button 3.7:1, footer links 4.0:1,
  card links 3.6:1). Now:
  - olive and anchovy buttons darken 14% towards Brine instead (5.5:1 and
    6.5:1)
  - cards and Services jump cards fade only their image
  - footer links underline
  - the homepage header no longer inherits the dark hero's link fade
- **Skip link.** A "Skip to content" link is the first tab stop on every page
  with a header. It is hidden until focused and jumps to `#main`. On the
  homepage, where the header sits inside the hero, it jumps to the hero copy.
  Its text is in Keystatic (Settings → Header → Skip link).
- **Reflow at 320px.** The Contact headline ("conversation.") was 36px wider
  than a 320px screen. On phones it now scales with the viewport below 372px
  and stays at the artboard's 52px above that.
- **Menu button target.** The phone MENU button is now at least 44 × 44px
  (it was 39px wide). The visible label does not move.

## Screen reader notes

These come from the accessibility tree Chromium exposes (what VoiceOver and
NVDA read), checked for each component. **They have not yet been confirmed
with VoiceOver on macOS and iOS or NVDA on Windows:** that needs a person on
those devices. See _Still to do_.

- **Landmarks.** Every page has `main` and a `contentinfo` footer with a
  "Footer navigation". Inner pages have a `banner` header with "Main"
  navigation. On the homepage the header sits inside the hero section, so it
  is announced as navigation within the hero region rather than as a banner.
  The design places it in the hero; this is allowed by WCAG and is noted here
  as a known structure.
- **Headings.** One `h1` per page. Section headers read with their number,
  such as "01 Selected work".
- **Hero mark.** A group "Light an ingredient" with three toggle buttons:
  "Olive · Plan", "Anchovy · Build", "Pepper · Grow". Focus lights a piece,
  so the focused button reads as pressed; Enter or Space turns it off and on.
  The mark image has its alt text from Keystatic; the coloured overlays are
  hidden from the tree.
- **Branch strip.** Three links whose names include the branch, its line and
  its services. The `01 / Olive` labels and EXPLORE are hidden as decoration.
- **Rotator.** A region "In their words" with a heading. Each slide is a
  figure named "Testimonial 1 of 4: Carmelo Vella". The dots are a group
  "Choose a testimonial" whose current dot has `aria-current`. A polite live
  region announces a slide only when the visitor picks it, never on autoplay.
  PAUSE and PLAY are a single button labelled "Pause the testimonials" or
  "Play the testimonials". Under reduced motion there is no autoplay and no
  button. Inactive slides are `inert`.
- **Disclosures.** A button "Show all 5 items" with `aria-expanded` and
  `aria-controls`. The item numbers are hidden; the list is an ordered list.
- **Filters.** A group "Filter projects by branch" of toggle buttons with
  `aria-pressed`. A polite live region announces "Showing 2 of 3 projects"
  after each change, cleared first so a repeat count is announced again.
- **Pillars.** Each pillar is a heading containing a button, such as
  "01 / Olive Plan 4 things we did", with `aria-expanded`.
- **Mobile menu.** MENU has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. The open menu
  is a modal `dialog` named "Menu" with CLOSE, the nav, Book a chat and the
  email address.
- **Contact form.** Every field has a visible label. Optional fields say
  "optional" in the label. Hints and errors are tied by `aria-describedby`,
  invalid fields get `aria-invalid`, and the summary is a status message. The
  needs and preferred-contact choices are fieldsets with legends. A sending
  error is an alert; success moves focus to the confirmation.
- **All-caps labels.** Some labels are typed in capitals (the design's mono
  labels), such as "IN THEIR WORDS". Screen readers read these as words, but
  some voices spell out short capitalised words. Worth listening for in the
  VoiceOver and NVDA runs.

## Images

- The mark carries meaning in the hero and the header lockup, and has alt text
  edited in Keystatic. Decorative repeats (band marks, ingredient
  illustrations, mark pieces, the engraved mask) have empty alt or are hidden.
- Case-study main and gallery images require alt text in Keystatic: the field
  is required and the build fails without it. Card covers sit inside a link
  that already names the project, so their alt is optional and usually empty.

## Target size

- Every target meets WCAG 2.2's 24 × 24px minimum (2.5.8), checked by axe's
  `target-size` rule on every page. Footer and desktop nav links are 15–17px
  tall text links that pass by the rule's spacing exception: nothing else sits
  within 24px of them.
- Primary touch targets are 44px on touch screens (`any-pointer: coarse`):
  buttons, contact choices, filter chips, rotator dots and PAUSE, disclosure
  triggers and the MENU button. With a mouse, buttons keep the artboard's 40px
  height.

## Motion, zoom and reflow

- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing transitions or animates: the mark
  switches without scale or fade and the rotator does not autoplay.
- Every page reflows at 320px without horizontal scrolling, which also covers
  zoom to 400% on a 1280px window (and 200% comfortably).
